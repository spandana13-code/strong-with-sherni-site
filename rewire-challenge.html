The Rewire Challenge

A 60-day lifestyle reset — not a sprint. Built around five pillars: sleep, mindfulness, nutrition, movement, and digital balance. Small habits, tracked daily, done consistently, until they stop feeling like a challenge and start feeling like your life.

The Five Pillars

What Rewire actually rewires

Every habit on the tracker touches one of these — nothing random, nothing just for the sake of doing more.

Sleep

7h 15m target — a full sleep cycle, not a random number

Mindfulness

Daily meditation, journaling, and breathwork

Nutrition

Meal logging, hydration, and mindful check-ins

Movement

10,000 steps daily + 5-7 training sessions a week

Digital Balance

Max 1 hour/day of social apps
